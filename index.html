Phase 1 How we build it

We do the technical work. You just use it.

Your course material is processed on our own secure computers with locally run tools. It is not sent to outside AI services.

Gather

We collect your course files, working with your platform contractor.

Transcribe and read

Videos get timestamped transcripts. PDFs and scans get their text read.

Tag and index

Topics, keywords and phrases on every file and section.

Check by hand

A person checks every file, transcript, tag, playback and search.

Go live

One secure link. You test it with us before anything is final.

New material later

Send us the files. We process, tag, add them to folders and check they can be found and opened.

If something breaks

Tell us. We investigate and fix it. Anything bigger, like moving servers, is explained and agreed first.

Safe by design

Login checks on every file, HTTPS, encrypted storage and backups, two-step login for admins, virus scans, tested restores.

Tested before it goes live

  • Every approved answer, and rephrased versions of each question
  • Off-topic and unclear questions, which it should politely decline
  • Trick messages that try to make it break its rules
  • It can never show private client conversations
